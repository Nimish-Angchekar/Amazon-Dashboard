# Key Insights

* Analyzed **1.43M Amazon products across 248 categories**, with an average rating of **4.00**.
* **Kitchen & Dining** recorded the highest recent purchase activity at approximately **10.4M units**.
* The catalog shows a wide range of pricing, with an average selling price of **$43.38**.
* **Moderate discounts dominate** the catalog, while products with discounts above 50% represent a relatively small share.
* Higher-priced products do not consistently receive higher ratings, indicating that **price alone is not a strong indicator of customer satisfaction**.
* Approximately **1.15M products** have ratings between **4.0 and 5.0**, showing generally positive customer feedback.
* Around **0.71M products** are rated between **4.5 and 5.0**, representing the largest rating segment.
* Only around **0.15M products** have ratings below **3.0**, indicating a relatively small proportion of low-rated products.
* Approximately **24.20% of products are discounted**, while the **bestseller share is around 0.6%**.
* The analysis highlights strong product diversity, concentrated purchase activity in leading categories, and generally positive customer ratings across the marketplace.

### Overall Takeaway

The analysis shows a **large and diverse Amazon product catalog**, where customer ratings remain generally positive, moderate discounts are common, and purchase activity is concentrated in selected categories.
